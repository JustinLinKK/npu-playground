# Third-party example provenance

The kernel inputs in this directory are reduced, formatting-adapted excerpts used to
exercise source analysis. The manifests and NumPy reference semantics are original to
this repository.

- NVIDIA CUDA Samples, pinned at `b7c5481c556c3fe98db060207ecaa41a4b9a9abc`,
  are distributed under the BSD-3-Clause license included in the upstream files.
- Triton tutorials, pinned at `64c974fda6bac26ec742e3e566e65848ba53b07f`,
  are distributed under the MIT license.
- AMD ROCm examples, pinned at `c3cae692de09d497d2bdb30a1bed41c1e9327312`,
  are distributed under the MIT license.

Each manifest records the precise upstream source location used as design provenance.
